Field checklist · Liebenwalde

Two assignments, one trip. Tick as you go (ticks reset if you reload, that's fine).

Before you start: tell someone where you are and when you'll be back. Location ON for photos. Every indicator: one wide shot + one close-up, hand or pen for scale.

A · Hydrologic indicators (due Wed 9/30)

Golden rule: the indicator must be ABOVE today's water or on dry ground. Under water = doesn't count.
Already have (from earlier photos): lichen line, water mark, buttressed base, secondary flow channel, drift lines/rafted debris, sediment deposition = 6. Optional 7th: hummocks at the stream.

Aim: 2 or more new ones = 8-9 total. For each, note WHERE (which spot, how far from the water).

B · Water Quality / eutrophication (due Sun 10/4)

Freshwater: stream pool, pond, canal or lake.

Bonus: one photo, two assignments

Duckweed stranded ABOVE the waterline = hydro (aquatic plants). A duckweed mat ON the water = eutrophication. Photograph both.

Send photos to Joule when you have signal, in any order. No signal: keep going, send later.
